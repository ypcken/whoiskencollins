About

It started with an Apple IIe in the late '80s. In 1993 I got my first Mac as a high school graduation gift. It had an 80MB hard drive, and my dad said it would last a lifetime. I filled it by the end of the summer.

That same year I downloaded the complete works of Shakespeare over a dial-up modem... just because I could. It took hours. Worth it.

From there it was Emerson College, my own web development firm, nonprofits, a couple of social networks, and eventually YPC Media, where I've done a little bit of everything: software, directories, CRMs, dialers, phone systems, apps, and a lot of WordPress.

Most of that work happened behind the scenes. That's fine by me. I like being the guy who makes the thing work so somebody else's business can look good.

Ai, in the right HUMAN hands

I love Ai as a tool and I'm not a fan of slop. But there's a third group besides the cheerleaders and the haters: people using it as one more tool to make great work. That's where I want to be.

The tools keep getting easier. The skill that's left is the story and the message. It still takes a human to come up with that.

Also, Ai is a yes man. It once "confirmed" a problem for me that didn't exist, and I almost went public with it. Verify before you bet your credibility on it.

A few true stories

Real ones. I checked.

The gossiping chatbot (2004)

When people were throwing pigs and poking each other on Facebook in the early 2000s, I wrote a Facebook app called "Talking Head." It was inspired by Max Headroom and ran on the A.L.I.C.E. chatbot and PHP. It learned from conversations... and started telling people things like "I don't know much about that, but Suzie says..." It got me in hot water with friends, and I shut it down. Ethics in Ai before it was a topic.

The Turduckin of audio players

My first car was a '79 Camaro with an 8-track. So naturally I ran a CD adapter into a cassette adapter into the 8-track. The Turduckin of audio players.

Breathing life into a dying CMS

Over a dozen years ago, we had 1,000+ customer sites on a CMS that wasn't built for phones. I made them all mobile-responsive. That gave the platform about another decade of life and kept a lot of small businesses looking current.

The first garden from seed

First year growing everything from seed. It barely produced. The cucumbers finally showed up in October. I saved the seeds to try again. Seed to seed.

Chewing ice

My better half hated that I chewed ice. I finally told her to stop telling me to stop, left the cup on my desk, and caught myself every time I reached for it. You can't app your way out of a habit. You have to want it and hear yourself do it.

The Ai slop pumpkin

For a 2026 pumpkin contest: a carved robot spilling real pumpkin guts into a bowl. Title: "Ai slop." I stand by it.
